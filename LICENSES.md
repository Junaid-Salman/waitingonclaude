# Third-party licences

WaitingOnClaude bundles or derives from the third-party material below. npm dependencies carry their own licences in `node_modules`.

## Word data

The Woordle answer and guess lists are built from SCOWL, filtered with LDNOOBW and FrequencyWords. Sources, versions, licences and the derivation rules are in [`party/data/LICENSES.md`](party/data/LICENSES.md). The word lists stay on the server and are never sent to the browser.
